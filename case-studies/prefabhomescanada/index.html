Case Study — PrefabHomesCanada

Acquisition Strategy & High-Ticket Lead Generation (Canada)

Context & Objective

PrefabHomesCanada was built as a performance-focused digital asset targeting high-intent commercial queries in the Canadian prefab and modular housing market.

The objective was to capture decision-stage traffic and structure a conversion-oriented user journey to validate lead generation potential within a high-ticket category.

Acquisition Strategy

1. Commercial Positioning & Research

  • Identified high-intent keywords (expandable homes, prefab homes Canada)
  • Focused on decision-driven and comparison-oriented queries
  • Prioritized transactional search intent opportunities

2. Funnel Architecture

  • Conversion-focused SEO structure
  • Comparison and decision-stage content frameworks
  • Strategic call-to-action placement
  • User journey optimization to reduce friction

3. Tracking & Behavioral Analysis

  • GA4 tracking implementation (behavioral events)
  • Scroll depth and engagement analysis
  • Data-driven iteration based on user signals

Technical Performance

Acquisition & Engagement (GA4)

Conversion Optimization

  • Headline and hook optimization to improve CTR
  • Reduced decision friction across the content flow
  • Structured comparison sections to support intent
  • Acquisition thinking aligned with ROI and cost-efficiency

Strategic Outcome

This project demonstrates the ability to structure an acquisition-focused digital asset in a high-ticket market by combining commercial SEO positioning, funnel logic, and data-driven optimization.

The approach leverages continuous analysis of key signals (CTR, engagement, behavioral events) to improve overall efficiency and support long-term lead generation scalability.

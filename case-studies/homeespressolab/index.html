SEO Case Study: HomeEspressoLab (Canada)

Role: SEO & Content Operator (end-to-end) · Market: Canada · Model: Editorial SEO + affiliate conversion

Overview

  • Project: HomeEspressoLab
  • Focus: High-intent commercial queries (“best”, “under $500”, “for beginners”)
  • Measurement: GA4 event tracking + outbound clicks

Context & Objectives

  • Build a scalable SEO asset from scratch in a competitive niche.
  • Target decision-stage searches and optimize content for conversion paths.
  • Measure user behavior and iterate using real data.

Strategy & Execution

1) Keyword research (commercial intent)

Prioritized intent clarity over volume, focusing on buying-stage queries.

2) Content architecture

Pillar pages per intent cluster with internal linking to reinforce topical relevance and crawlability.

3) CTR optimization

  • Comparison tables + “Quick picks” above the fold
  • Editorial tone (no aggressive sales language)
  • Scannable layouts designed for decision-making

4) Technical SEO & performance

  • Core Web Vitals improvements (LCP, CLS, TTFB)
  • Image optimization + lazy loading
  • Caching strategy aligned with UX and conversion paths

5) Analytics

  • GA4 implemented with event tracking for engagement and outbound clicks
  • Data-driven iteration based on real user behavior

Results (early-stage)

  • Core pages indexed and ranking on commercial-intent queries.
  • 1,600+ GA4 events recorded in the initial growth phase.
  • Measured outbound click activity and first affiliate revenue.

Stack

  • WordPress · GA4 · Google Search Console
  • Content structure + internal linking strategy

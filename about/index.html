What I Do

  • Shopify store launches (clean, fast, conversion-ready)
  • WooCommerce performance optimization (Core Web Vitals, checkout speed)
  • Platform audits and architecture decisions
  • Technical consulting for founders and small teams

How I Work

I don’t sell templates or magic formulas. I work from real constraints: traffic sources, performance metrics, business stage, and technical debt.

  1. Audit speed, UX, and funnel data
  2. Recommend the platform that fits your stage (not my preference)
  3. Optimize or launch with minimal overhead
  4. Measure and iterate

Why This Site Exists

This site exists to explain eCommerce platforms honestly. Shopify is excellent for speed and time-to-market. WooCommerce is better when control and customization matter.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you sign up through them, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Important: I only recommend tools I would personally use for a client. No paid placements. No fear-based marketing.

When I Recommend Shopify

  • You want to launch fast
  • You don’t want to manage hosting or updates
  • You’re validating an idea or MVP
  • You prefer simplicity over deep customization

When I Don’t

  • You need complex logic or heavy customization
  • You want full control over performance
  • You already operate a content-heavy WordPress ecosystem

About

I help businesses make clear, practical decisions about their eCommerce stack — without hype, pressure, or overengineering.

I’m an independent eCommerce performance consultant focused on speed, clarity, and long-term sustainability. My work sits at the intersection of technical performance, platform selection, and business reality.

My Approach

Not every business needs the same tools. Shopify is excellent in some situations. WooCommerce is better in others.

My role isn’t to push a platform — it’s to explain tradeoffs honestly so founders and teams can choose what fits their stage, resources, and goals.

Why This Site Exists

This website exists to publish clear, experience-based guidance on eCommerce platforms, performance, and setup decisions.

Some pages contain affiliate links. When they do, it’s because the tool or platform is something I would recommend regardless of commission.

Important: I don’t sell Shopify plans, I don’t collect phone numbers, and I don’t run aggressive funnels.

If you sign up through an affiliate link, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Who I Work With

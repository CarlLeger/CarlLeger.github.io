What I focus on

I’m an independent consultant and editor focused on WordPress and WooCommerce performance, hosting decisions and technical SEO. This site combines practical guides with a professional service offering for website owners and teams.

  • Core Web Vitals, server response time and application bottlenecks.
  • Hosting requirements, operating models and recurring costs.
  • Checkout sessions, cache boundaries and release validation.
  • Indexability, internal links, canonicals and template structure.
  • Consent-aware measurement of meaningful site interactions.

How I work

My approach starts with a baseline, a clear problem and a prioritised plan. Staging, validation and rollback should form part of the implementation discussion. A specific result is not promised before the cause and scope are understood.

For hosting content, official documentation provides the basis for product facts. The guides distinguish those facts from editorial judgement and do not claim controlled benchmark results or firsthand testing that has not been documented.

Anonymised technical examples

These summaries focus on technical choices from independent web projects. Identifying details and commercial information are withheld.

  • Editorial website: technical structure and measurement
  • Website review: performance and validation
  • Content website: navigation and template consistency

Editorial independence and affiliate disclosure

Some guides include marked affiliate links. If you purchase through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A provider’s suitability depends on the site’s requirements, complete cost and the team operating it.

Documentation links are ordinary source links. No star rating, certification, testimonial or guaranteed business result is used as a substitute for evidence.

FAQ (Canada)

Is Teachable worth it in Canada?

It can be, especially if your priority is launching quickly and focusing on marketing. If your strategy is SEO-heavy, use your main site for content + authority, and Teachable for checkout/delivery.

Which platform is best for SEO?

Often: your own fast domain for content, then the platform as conversion/delivery. If you want more platform-side flexibility, Thinkific usually feels less restrictive.

Should I start on a platform or build custom?

If you need speed, start on a platform and validate. If you already have traffic and a clear product, a hybrid (site for SEO, platform for delivery) scales best long-term.

Can I switch platforms later?

Usually yes, but migrations take time (content, students, offers, email sequences). Keeping acquisition on your own domain reduces lock-in.
